Build Business Relationships on Trust

TradeNest uses multi-layered business verification, risk checks, supplier performance tracking, standardized Trust Scores, and authentic buyer feedback to help businesses make more informed sourcing decisions and reduce counterparty risk.

  • Business Verification: Validation of business registration documents, licenses, and key enterprise information.
  • Trust Score: A dynamic score assessing operational history, compliance, transaction volume, and service consistency.
  • Supplier Performance: Historical data on on-time delivery rates, order accuracy, and fulfillment reliability.
  • Buyer Feedback: Authentic ratings and detailed performance reviews from verified purchasing entities.
  • Risk Monitoring: Ongoing assessment of entity status, license validity, and compliance updates over time.

RFQ & Quotation Workflow

A simple, structured progression from initial procurement request to order execution.

Step 1

Buyer Requirement

Buyer defines sourcing criteria, product specs, quantities, and target timeline.

↓
Step 2

Request Quote

Buyer generates and dispatches a formal RFQ to select suppliers or market network.

↓
Step 3

Supplier Receives RFQ

Matching vendors review detailed specifications, technical terms, and delivery schedules.

↓
Step 4

Supplier Sends Offer

Supplier prepares and issues a comprehensive commercial proposal and quotation.

↓
Step 5

Buyer Compares Offers

Buyer compares incoming bids, lead times, warranty terms, and pricing metrics.

↓
Step 6

Buyer Selects Supplier

Buyer awards the contract, negotiates final terms, and confirms seller selection.

↓
Step 7

Order

Official purchase order is generated, kicking off production, payment, and delivery.

Supplier Collaboration

TradeNest provides powerful collaboration capabilities that allow suppliers to form alliances and partner with other verified vendors when a complex buyer requirement involves multiple distinct product categories, high-volume quantities exceeding single-factory capacity, or specialized complementary capabilities.

This co-sourcing framework empowers mid-sized suppliers to collectively bid on large enterprise tenders, ensuring buyers receive comprehensive solutions without breaking their procurement workflow.
