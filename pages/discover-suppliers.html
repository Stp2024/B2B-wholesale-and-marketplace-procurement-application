Understand Supplier Trust Score

Trust Score is an indicator designed to help buyers evaluate available supplier information and performance signals.

Sample Evaluated Metric Trust Score: 92/100

Key Indicators Analyzed:

  • Business Verification: Validation of business credentials, documentation, and regulatory registration.
  • Completed Orders: Track record of successfully processed wholesale purchase transactions.
  • On-Time Delivery: Historical performance regarding agreed shipment and fulfillment timelines.
  • Customer Feedback: Verified ratings and reviews submitted by previous buyers.
  • Response Time: Average speed in addressing RFQs, inquiry messages, and quote requests.

Note: Trust Score is one source of information and does not guarantee supplier performance. Buyers should complete their own due diligence prior to placing orders.
