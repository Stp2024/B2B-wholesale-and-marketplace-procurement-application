Understanding the TradeNest Trust Score

Trust Score is an indicator designed to help buyers evaluate supplier reliability using available business and performance information.

Note: The Trust Score serves as an informational tool calculated from historical data points. It does not guarantee supplier performance or outcome.

On-Time Delivery

Tracks historical fulfillment speeds and adherence to agreed shipment timelines.

Completed Orders

Evaluates the volume and consistency of successfully processed business transactions.

Customer Feedback

Aggregates post-transaction ratings and verified client evaluations.

Response Time

Measures the speed and responsiveness of supplier communication regarding inquiries.

Business Verification

Confirms corporate registration credentials, documentation, and operational operationality.

Compare Suppliers Before You Decide

TradeNest enables enterprise procurement teams to analyze and compare operational metrics directly from verified vendor profiles. Make informed sourcing evaluations based on objective, standardized parameters:

  • Product Availability: Check real-time inventory and catalog depth.
  • Wholesale Pricing: Review tier-based bulk pricing structures.
  • Trust Score: Measure objective reliability ratings based on historical operational data.
  • Ratings & Feedback: Evaluate historical buyer reviews and ratings.
  • Delivery Time: Compare standard lead times and fulfillment speed.
  • MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity): Find suppliers aligned with your purchasing volume.
  • Response Time: Benchmark supplier communication speed.

What You Can Explore on a Supplier Profile

Detailed insight into operational capabilities provided across vendor pages.

Business Information

Corporate background, operating history, legal structure, and registered office locations.

Verification Status

Platform verification checkmarks verifying legal identity and operational legitimacy.

Product Catalog

Comprehensive list of available SKUs, specifications, technical sheets, and product lines.

Trust Score

Calculated reliability rating derived from performance history on the platform.

Ratings & Feedback

Verified ratings and qualitative assessments left by previous commercial procurement buyers.

Location & Logistics

Geographic manufacturing bases, dispatch locations, and export coverage zones.

Minimum Order Quantity

Clear baseline volume requirements (MOQ) for initiating production or wholesale orders.

Delivery Information

Estimated dispatch timelines, supported shipping terms (FOB, CIF, EXW), and courier partnerships.

Response Time

Historical average timeframe for responding to Requests for Quotation (RFQs) and inquiries.

Business Performance

Standardized indicators regarding order completion history and operational consistency.

Communication Options

Direct inquiry tools and communication channels accessible according to platform user permissions.
