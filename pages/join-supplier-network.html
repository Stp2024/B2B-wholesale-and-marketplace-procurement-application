Supplier Responsibilities

Maintaining marketplace integrity, transparent trading, and professional service standards.

  • Provide accurate business information during onboarding and ongoing reviews.
  • Provide accurate product information, technical specs, and imagery.
  • Keep product availability and pricing information updated where required.
  • Respond to buyer requirements and formal quotation requests honestly.
  • Follow applicable laws, commercial codes, and trade regulations.
  • Fulfill accepted orders according to agreed contract terms and timelines.
  • Maintain professional and timely communication with marketplace users.
  • Provide required verification documentation upon administrative request.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries regarding supplier enrollment and marketplace practices.

1. How can I join TradeNest as a supplier?

Create a TradeNest account, select the supplier role and complete the required business information and verification process.

2. What information is required?

The platform may require account information, business information, address details and verification documents depending on the supplier type and applicable requirements.

3. Can I list multiple products?

The supplier platform is designed to support product catalog management and multiple product listings.

4. Can suppliers receive buyer RFQs?

The platform is designed to allow relevant suppliers to receive and respond to buyer requirements and RFQs.

5. Can suppliers collaborate with other suppliers?

TradeNest can provide supplier collaboration tools for requirements that involve multiple suppliers or complementary capabilities.

6. Does joining guarantee sales?

No. Joining a marketplace does not guarantee orders or sales. Supplier opportunities depend on buyer requirements, product relevance, pricing, availability and other factors.

7. How does supplier verification work?

Submitted business information may be reviewed through verification and risk-checking processes before relevant supplier access is provided.
